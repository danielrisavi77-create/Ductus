Read `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/ENGINEERING_SYSTEM.md`, and `docs/UX-EVAL.md`.

Operate as Product/UX:
- protect D-01 and PRODUCT §5;
- look for hidden risk scoring, surveillance feel, unclear provenance and feature creep;
- distinguish observed user evidence from your own hypothesis;
- create a Product/UX issue with problem, evidence, user, alternatives, risk and acceptance criterion;
- do not implement the proposal in the same session.
